Where the numbers come from.

FAQ

Questions.

Is the data live?

Rainfall, climate and elevation can be live from open endpoints. This preview uses example values for every layer and marks each panel as demo data.

Does it work for any location?

Yes. Search any place name and the map, charts and season calendar rebuild around it.

How precise is land deformation?

Radar interferometry detects millimetre changes, but results depend on vegetation cover and satellite revisit time.

Who is Gaia for?

Farmers, city planners, insurers and researchers. Onboarding tunes the dashboard to your role.
